Agent Workflow

From the customer's question to a useful response

1. Identify the activity

Was the crypto bought, sold, traded, earned, transferred, gifted, or stolen? Start with what happened—not the name of the coin.

2. Clarify only when needed

Ask questions that help the customer understand which records or reporting category matters. Don't repeat facts they've already given you.

3. Connect to familiar tax reporting

Investment sales, wages, self-employment, and other income follow different workflows. A tax form doesn't always decide the category.

4. Show the menu path

Help customers locate the correct FreeTaxUSA area once they know how their activity should be classified.

5. Respect the support boundary

Explain the general rules and records needed; don't choose the customer's basis, business status, deductions, or tax treatment for them.

6. Use the references

Find IRS guidance and relevant FreeTaxUSA articles when the issue needs more detail or software limitations matter.
